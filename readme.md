I am a **Medical Microbiologist (M.Sc.)**, **Bioinformatician**, and **Published Clinical Researcher** currently serving as a **Tutor in the Department of Microbiology at MGM Medical College & Institute of Health Sciences, Vashi, Navi Mumbai**. My research and engineering focus on **Infectious Disease**, **Immunology**, **Cell Biology**, and **Multi-Omics Machine Learning Workflows**.

By combining over **5 years of hands-on clinical diagnostic and academic experience** (spanning Bacteriology, Parasitology, Mycology, Serology, TB, and Molecular Diagnostics) with modern computational biology, I bridge wet-lab clinical evidence and systematic literature synthesis with reproducible dry-lab automation.

My work spans **first-author peer-reviewed clinical research on Antimicrobial Resistance (VRE)** and **PROSPERO-registered systematic reviews on opportunistic protozoan pathogens (*Pentatrichomonas hominis*)**, alongside end-to-end bioinformatics pipelines for **whole-genome $k$-mer AMR phenotype prediction**, **Compositional Data Analysis (CoDa) in gut dysbiosis**, **scRNA-seq intercellular signaling**, and **Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) for protein-protein interactions**.

> [!TIP]
> 🔬 **Translational Research Highlight:** Co-designed and completed a global systematic review (*2000–2026*) investigating ***Pentatrichomonas hominis* as an opportunistic pathogen in immunocompromised hosts** ([PROSPERO `CRD420261422321`](https://www.crd.york.ac.uk/PROSPERO/view/CRD420261422321), *submitted for publication*), paired directly with an open-source multi-omics detection pipeline ([`phominis-multiomics-pipeline`](https://github.com/ritsushah/phominis-multiomics-pipeline)).

### 🚀 Featured Bioinformatics & ML Pipelines

* 🦠 **[`phominis-multiomics-pipeline`](https://github.com/ritsushah/phominis-multiomics-pipeline)** — *Opportunistic Pathogen Surveillance*  
  End-to-end multi-omics pipeline for detecting and characterizing *Pentatrichomonas hominis* as a potential opportunistic pathogen in immunocompromised gut microbiomes—featuring custom genome/marker databases and automated profiling workflows complementing [PROSPERO `CRD420261422321`](https://www.crd.york.ac.uk/PROSPERO/view/CRD420261422321).
* 🧬 **[`protozoa-bacterial-ibd-dysbiosis`](https://github.com/ritsushah/protozoa-bacterial-ibd-dysbiosis)** — *Quantitative Metagenomics & Nextflow DSL2*  
  Reproducible metagenomic workflow modeling intestinal protozoa–bacterial interactions in Inflammatory Bowel Disease (IBD) dysbiosis using Compositional Data Analysis (CoDa) and leakage-free machine learning.
* 💊 **[`amr-kmer-pipeline`](https://github.com/ritsushah/amr-kmer-pipeline)** — *Computational Antimicrobial Resistance*  
  Proof-of-concept (POC) machine learning pipeline for predicting Antimicrobial Resistance (AMR) phenotypes directly from bacterial whole-genome $k$-mer representations using Python, PyTorch, and Docker, building on clinical AMR surveillance research.
* 🩸 **[`Gfi1_AML_Signaling_Mapping`](https://github.com/ritsushah/Gfi1_AML_Signaling_Mapping)** — *Cancer Transcriptomics & Single-Cell Omics*  
  Computational pipeline mapping *Gfi1*-regulated intercellular signaling and bone marrow niche remodeling in Acute Myeloid Leukemia (AML) by integrating bulk TCGA cohorts with scRNA-seq (`GSE116256`) datasets.
* 🕸️ **[`HippoActInteract`](https://github.com/ritsushah/HippoActInteract)** — *Deep Learning in Structural & Systems Biology*  
  Graph Neural Network (GNN) architecture designed to predict and categorize physical protein-protein interactions between Hippo signaling and actin cytoskeleton pathway proteins.
